Invoice Intake

Automated invoice processing — from supplier email to finance-ready queue. Powered by Power Automate, AI extraction, and SharePoint.

Overview

Invoice Intake transforms the accounts payable process from a manual email folder into an automated workflow. Supplier invoices land in a shared mailbox, are automatically extracted by AI, queued in SharePoint, and routed through approval — all without manual data entry.

Detection Methods

Option 1 — Simple (Outlook Rule + Folder)

Detect emails with PDF attachment and keywords (Invoice, Tax Invoice, Bill). Good for quick deployment and low volume, but no extraction and manual processing.

Option 2 — Recommended (Power Automate + AI Builder)

When email arrives: check for PDF attachment, run AI invoice model, extract key fields, create queue item, notify finance, track status.

Option 3 — Enterprise (Accounts Payable Automation)

Integrates SharePoint, Dataverse, Business Central / ERP with duplicate detection, PO matching, approval routing, exception handling, audit trail.

Security Controls

Supplier allow list, attachment malware scan, duplicate invoice detection, SPF/DKIM verification, alert on changed bank account details, alert on unusually large invoice amounts — reducing invoice fraud and business email compromise.
