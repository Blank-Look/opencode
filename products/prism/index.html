About Prism

Learning that bends light
in new directions.

Prism Academy is a progressive K–12 school where academic rigour meets creative exploration. Every student follows a core curriculum enriched with design thinking, digital fluency, and real-world projects.

🔭

Inquiry-Driven

Students learn by asking questions, not memorising answers. Every unit starts with a provocation, not a textbook.

🧩

Project-Based

Learning happens through making. From coding apps to building gardens, every student creates a portfolio of real work.

🌐

Globally Connected

Partnerships with schools in 12 countries. Every student completes a global collaboration project each year.

Our Programs

Designed for every stage
of curiosity.

From foundation years through senior pathways, each program balances core knowledge with hands-on innovation.

🌱

Discovery Years

K–2. Play-based inquiry, early literacy & numeracy, nature Immersion, and social-emotional learning.

🔬

Explorer Program

Years 3–6. Interdisciplinary projects, design thinking, coding fundamentals, and guided research.

⚡

Innovator Track

Years 7–9. Deep-dive electives, entrepreneurship, advanced digital media, and community impact projects.

🎯

Pathfinder Senior

Years 10–12. IB and alternative pathways, industry mentorship, capstone projects, and university partnerships.

🤖

AI & Digital Fluency

Across all years. Age-appropriate AI literacy, ethics, coding, robotics, and digital creation.

🌍

Global Citizen

Cross-cultural exchange, language immersion, sustainability action projects, and student-led conferences.
