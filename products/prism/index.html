Why Prism

Designed for the way
the world actually works.

The future belongs to those who can think critically, collaborate across disciplines, and create value. That's not a degree program — it's a mindset. And it's what we build, every day.

🧠

Inquiry, Not Instruction

Every unit begins with a question, not an answer. Students learn to frame problems, gather evidence, and construct arguments — skills that translate to any career, any field.

90% of students say they learn more deeply than in a traditional setting
🛠️

Build to Learn

Portfolios, not exams. Every student produces a body of work — apps, business plans, research papers, documentary films — that demonstrates real capability to universities and employers.

3+ major projects per student, per year
🌍

Global by Default

Partnerships with 40+ organisations across 12 countries. Every student completes at least one global collaboration project each year, working across time zones and cultures.

100% of students complete a global project before graduation
Our Programs

A pathway for every
stage of growth.

Each stage builds on the last — from foundational curiosity to capstone work that rivals professional-level output.

🌱

Discovery Years

Play-based inquiry, early literacy and numeracy, nature immersion, and social-emotional foundations. Where a love of learning begins.

🔬

Explorer Program

Interdisciplinary projects, design thinking, coding fundamentals, and guided research. Students learn to ask better questions and find their own answers.

⚡

Innovator Track

Deep-dive electives in AI, entrepreneurship, digital media, and social impact. Students specialise, lead teams, and take their first real-world briefs.

🎯

Pathfinder

Advanced pathways, industry mentorship, and a year-long capstone project. Graduates leave with a portfolio that opens doors.

🤖

AI & Digital Fluency

AI literacy, ethics, coding, and digital creation — embedded across every program level, not siloed into a single subject.

🌍

Global Citizen

Cross-cultural exchange, sustainability action projects, and student-led conferences. Learning that extends far beyond the classroom.
