SchoolApp

Everything a master's student needs — subjects, timetable, portal links, events, and resources. One hub, every device.

Overview

SchoolApp is a mobile-first student hub that aggregates everything a master's student needs into a single app-like interface. Subjects, timetable, quick links to Blackboard, Canvas, Student Portal, M365, and alumni network — plus what's on, deadlines, and support resources. Designed as a progressive web app so it works on Android, iOS, and desktop from one codebase.
