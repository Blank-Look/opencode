# Safety Plan

## 1. Pre-Travel

### Risk Assessment

All travel destinations are assessed against:

- FCO / DFAT travel advisory rating
- Historical natural disaster data (earthquakes, tsunamis, cyclones, floods)
- Political stability index
- Healthcare infrastructure quality
- Crime and security data

### Approval Workflow

1. Traveller submits itinerary with destination, dates, accommodation, contact details
2. System calculates destination risk score
3. Low risk → auto-approved
4. Medium risk → Travel Manager review
5. High risk → Head of Security approval required
6. Critical risk → Executive approval + travel insurance verification

### Pre-Departure Requirements

- Travel insurance certificate (must cover medical evacuation and repatriation)
- Emergency contact details (traveller + next of kin)
- In-country emergency numbers (embassy, local emergency services)
- Signed acknowledgement of destination risks and safety procedures

## 2. In-Travel Monitoring

### Check-In Cadence

| Risk Level | Check-In Frequency | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Weekly | Email link |
| Medium | Every 3 days | Email + SMS |
| High | Daily | SMS + Teams |
| Critical | Twice daily | SMS + phone call |

### Regional Monitoring

Live feeds monitor for events in traveller regions:

- Natural disasters (earthquakes, tsunamis, volcanic activity, severe weather)
- Political events (protests, elections, coups)
- Health alerts (disease outbreaks, pandemics)
- Transport disruptions (airport closures, strike action)
- Security incidents (terrorism, civil unrest)

## 3. Emergency Response

Refer to the [Response Plan](response-plan.md) for detailed activation procedures.

## 4. Repatriation

### Triggers

- Warning or Critical level incident in traveller region
- Medical emergency requiring evacuation
- Political unrest threatening traveller safety
- Natural disaster making accommodation unsafe

### Process

1. Head of Security authorises repatriation
2. Travel Manager coordinates with travel provider and insurance
3. Consular assistance engaged if passport/visa issues
4. Traveller receives repatriation plan with timeline
5. Post-arrival check-in and welfare assessment

## 5. Insurance

- All travellers must hold comprehensive travel insurance
- Insurance must cover: medical evacuation, repatriation, trip cancellation, personal liability
- Insurance details recorded in itinerary and verified before departure
- Emergency assistance hotline programmed into traveller's phone

## 6. Training

- All staff travelling internationally must complete Travel Safety Briefing
- Annual refresher training required
- Training covers: check-in procedures, emergency response, local awareness, insurance claims
