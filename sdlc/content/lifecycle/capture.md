---
title: 1. Capture
---

Capture the business requirement in a short, structured request rather than beginning with a large project document.

## Approach

The request should explain:

- the problem or opportunity
- the desired business outcome
- who will use or own the solution
- the systems and data involved
- known constraints
- expected timing
- acceptance criteria
- the expected project size

## Minimum Output

**AI Delivery Request**

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Problem | What issue or opportunity exists |
| Desired outcome | What success looks like |
| Users and stakeholders | Who will use or own the solution |
| Systems affected | What systems will be touched |
| Data classification | Sensitivity of data involved |
| Constraints | Known limitations or boundaries |
| Acceptance criteria | How success will be measured |
| Target environments | Where the solution will run |
| Estimated cost and effort | Rough order of magnitude |
| Business owner | Accountable business person |
| Technical owner | Accountable technical person |

## Procurement

If the initiative requires vendor procurement (software, SaaS, hardware, or services), start the [ICT Procurement process](https://blank-look.github.io/opencode/products/knowledgebase/docs/data-and-protection/procurement.html) in parallel with, or before, Gate 1. Procurement lead times often exceed delivery timelines and can block Gate 3 if not initiated early.

## Gate 1 — Proceed to Design?

**Decision:** Go, Hold, Rework or Stop

Approval should confirm that the requirement is understood, has an accountable owner and is worth investigating.
