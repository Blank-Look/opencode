Capture the business requirement in a short, structured request rather than beginning with a large project document.

Approach

The request should explain:

  • the problem or opportunity
  • the desired business outcome
  • who will use or own the solution
  • the systems and data involved
  • known constraints
  • expected timing
  • acceptance criteria
  • the expected project size

Minimum Output

AI Delivery Request

Field Description
Problem What issue or opportunity exists
Desired outcome What success looks like

Target environments Where the solution will run
Estimated cost and effort Rough order of magnitude
Business owner Accountable business person
Technical owner Accountable technical person

Procurement

If the initiative requires vendor procurement (software, SaaS, hardware, or services), start the ICT Procurement process in parallel with, or before, Gate 1. Procurement lead times often exceed delivery timelines and can block Gate 3 if not initiated early.
