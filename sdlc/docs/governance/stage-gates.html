Simplified Stage Gates Summary

The lifecycle includes five stage gates:

  • Gate 1 — Intake: Confirms the requirement has an accountable owner and is worth investigating. Decision: Go, Hold, Rework or Stop.
  • Gate 2 — Governance: Confirms AI use is appropriate, sensitive information is protected, and agent boundaries are defined.
  • Gate 3 — Design: Technical owner reviews the generated prompt and, for medium/large work, the solution design, threat model, data flows and integration approach.
  • Gate 4 — Release: Confirms acceptance criteria met, checks passed, risks accepted, rollback proven, and business/technical owners agree.
  • Gate 5 — Operate: Operational owner accepts the service, support responsibilities, documentation, monitoring, known risks, ongoing costs and maintenance requirements.
