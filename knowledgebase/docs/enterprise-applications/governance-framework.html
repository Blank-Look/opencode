Governance Operating Model

The Enterprise Applications function operates under a defined governance framework that clarifies accountability, establishes consistent practices, and supports scalable delivery.

Functional Accountability

Function Accountable For
Enterprise Applications – Enablement Business analysis governance, stakeholder alignment, delivery governance, change readiness, operational adoption
Enterprise Applications – Data Platform engineering, integrations, technical standards, platform operations, data capability
Business Stakeholders Business ownership, prioritisation input, business validation, UAT, operational adoption
Vendors & Delivery Partners Technical delivery, technical quality assurance, technical testing
Enterprise Applications Leadership Governance oversight, prioritisation governance, escalation management, capability maturity

Governance Principles

  • Clear ownership and accountability
  • Governance-driven prioritisation
  • Business analysis before solutioning
  • Early stakeholder engagement
  • Operational readiness before implementation
  • Defined escalation pathways
  • Delivery transparency and visibility
  • Continuous improvement
  • Risk-based decision making
  • Scalable governance practices

Document Hierarchy

The Enterprise Applications function maintains a suite of governance artefacts:

Document Purpose
Operating Model & Governance Framework Governance, ownership, accountability, operating principles
Service Catalogue & Engagement Guide Services, engagement pathways, stakeholder expectations
Business Analysis Standards & Templates Requirements and business analysis standards
UAT Standards & Templates User Acceptance Testing standards and practices
Platform Ownership Register Platform ownership accountability
Delivery Reporting Standards Delivery reporting and governance requirements
Change & Enablement Artefacts Readiness, communication, adoption standards

Governance Intake Model

Enterprise Applications accepts requests through defined governance pathways aligned to the sensitivity and impact of the request. Low-sensitivity requests follow a streamlined path; high-sensitivity requests require full governance review.
