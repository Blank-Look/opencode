# Technology Roadmap

The COO's Technology Roadmap outlines the strategic direction for ICT across the School, shifting focus from foundational security maturity to business-value-driven outcomes — with AI adoption as the flagship priority.

## Strategic Shift

Mature technology domains (Devices, Identity, Networks, Systems) are moving to a **Continuous Improvement** model. These areas have strong alignment with Zero Trust and Essential Eight and now require optimisation rather than foundational change.

The next phase focuses on domains at a strategic decision point: **Applications and Data**.

## AI as the Driver

Rather than starting a standalone data governance project, the roadmap treats **AI (Microsoft Copilot)** as the driver. AI success requires better data governance, quality, access control, and asset management — delivering AI gives leverage to achieve these outcomes.

## Key Objectives (2026–2027)

### 1. App and Data Practices in the Era of AI

Establish simple, broad data controls based on sensitivity:
- **Sensitive** (CIA = High)
- **Not Sensitive**

Avoid over-classification. Treat Zero Trust as the default design principle for applications and data access.

### 2. Unified Asset Management

A single school-wide view of assets covering:
- Applications (including OAuth apps)
- Data stores, shared mailboxes, Teams
- Devices and network infrastructure

Class/type-based lifecycle management including:
- Access reviews
- Attestations
- Security posture monitoring (Defender, firmware, Secure Score)
- Clearly defined asset ownership
- Automation and rules tied to sensitivity and risk

### 3. Identity Boundaries for Cohorts

Clear access policies for non-staff cohorts: Students, Associates, Adjuncts, and others — ensuring appropriate identity boundaries.
